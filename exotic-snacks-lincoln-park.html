What is on the snack wall?

Urban Alchemist’s Curious shelf brings imported packaged snacks into our Lincoln Park shop at 2219 N Clybourn Ave, Unit A. The selection is organized by country, with snacks from Japan, Korea, Mexico, Thailand, India and the Philippines among the places represented.

Arrivals change. This page describes the collection rather than a live inventory: a country, brand or flavor may not be available on the day you visit. Call the shop if you have a particular item in mind.

Follow your curiosity

Start with flavor

Sweet, salty, sour or spicy? Naming a familiar flavor gives you a starting point for exploring something unfamiliar.

Look at the package

A familiar brand can have different flavors or ingredients in different markets. Check the actual package rather than assuming it is the same as a version you know.

A few good questions before choosing

  • What are the ingredients and allergen statements on this package?
  • Does it meet my dietary requirements? Look for a specific label rather than inferring from the flavor or country.
  • What is the best-before date, and how should it be stored?
  • Is this a regular arrival or part of a changing selection?

If a label is unclear, ask to read the available labeling before deciding. We cannot guarantee that every item is suitable for a particular allergy or diet.
