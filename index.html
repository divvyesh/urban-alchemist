How do I order delivery?

Choose a product and open it on Quickvee. Select the exact flavor or option, choose your quantity and add it to your cart there. Enter your address and review the order before paying.

Can you deliver to my address?

Quickvee checks delivery availability for your address. It also shows the current delivery fees, ordering hours and available fulfillment options before checkout.

Will I get the exact flavor in the photo?

Product photos show a representative option from the store listing. Open the product to check the available flavors, sizes, ingredients and prices, then select the exact item you want.

Can I still visit the shop?

Yes. Find us at 2219 N Clybourn Ave, Unit A, in Lincoln Park, Chicago. Check current hours on Google or call (312) 375-4448 before making a special trip.

How old do I need to be?

Urban Alchemist is an adults-only store for customers 21 and over. Valid ID is required. Review any additional delivery verification requirements on Quickvee.
