Check hours before you leave

For the latest listed hours and holiday changes, check our Google Business Profile. Call (312) 375-4448 to confirm before a late or special trip.

Finding the door

Look for Unit A at 2219 N Clybourn Avenue, on the same block as Pequod’s Pizza. Check posted restrictions and payment signs if using street parking; availability varies.

If you need accessibility details or help locating the entrance, call before your visit so we can answer your specific question.

What to expect

You can start with a question, an ingredient, or simply a look around. Explore the imported snack collection and tea and botanical shelves. There is no obligation to buy.

This website is a guide to the shop and an educational journal. It does not provide online ordering or real-time stock information.
