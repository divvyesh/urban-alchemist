Straight answers

Ask the
Alchemist.

The questions people actually ask at the counter.

Where exactly are you, and what's nearby?

2219 N Clybourn Ave, Unit A, Chicago, IL 60614, in Lincoln Park. We're two doors up from the Pequod's Pizza line at 2207, about half a mile from the DePaul University campus, and a short walk from Webster Place.

Walkable from Lincoln Park and Bucktown, a short ride from the Loop, and there's street parking on Clybourn. If you're waiting on a deep-dish table, you have time.

What do you actually sell?

Our shelves bring together wellness and personal-care formats, teas and botanicals, and rare imported snacks and drinks. In-store selection may differ. Browse our current online picks or call about a specific item.

The feeling menu is a way to browse by taste, occasion and routine, so you do not need to know a brand before you walk in.

Where can I buy Lion's Mane, Reishi, Cordyceps or Chaga in Lincoln Park?

Start here at 2219 N Clybourn Ave, or explore our online selection. Current functional-seltzer listings include formulas naming culinary and functional mushrooms. Check the exact ingredients and available options on Quickvee, or call about other in-store formats.

A mushroom name alone does not describe a complete product formula. Read the package for the exact item you are considering.

Do you have Japanese, Korean or Mexican snacks?

Yes. The snack wall is sorted by country: Japan, Korea, Mexico, Thailand, India, the Philippines, the United Kingdom, Germany, Brazil and Nigeria, and it changes as things come in.

It is the reason a lot of people first walk through the door, and it is a two-minute detour if you are already on Clybourn waiting for a table.

Is there parking? What's the closest stop?

There is street parking on Clybourn, and the Webster Place lot is a short walk. The 74 Fullerton and 73 Armitage buses both put you within a few blocks, and the Fullerton stop on the Red, Brown and Purple lines is walkable from the DePaul side.

Coming from Bucktown or Wrightwood, it is a straight shot down Clybourn.

What are your hours?

Check current hours on Google or call ahead. Call (312) 375-4448 if you're making a late trip.

I've never been in a shop like this. Where do I start?

With a feeling, not a product. Say "I want to focus," or "I want to wind down," or "no idea, show me something." We hear all three every day.

You don't need to know a brand or an ingredient name. That's our job.

Is this the real thing, or is it fake?

Check the manufacturer, sealed package and available documentation for the exact item. Ask us to help read its label or any batch report supplied with it.

Product types differ: a food label, device manual and supplement package answer different questions. We can help you find the relevant information.

What is a certificate of analysis?

A laboratory report showing the results of specified tests on a particular sample or batch. Check the batch number, laboratory, date and tests actually performed.

Where a report is supplied, compare it with the exact package. A report answers the questions it tested; it is not a guarantee of a particular benefit.

Are the mushrooms psychedelic?

Lion’s Mane, Cordyceps, Reishi and Chaga are the culinary and functional species discussed in our field guides. Complete retail formulas vary, so read the exact package rather than relying on a mushroom name or shelf category.

If an ingredient or label is unclear, ask us to help identify it before you choose.

Can you tell me what to take for my condition?

No, and we'd be suspicious of any shop that said yes. We're a retail store, not a clinic, and nothing we sell is intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease.

What we can do is tell you what something is, where it came from, how it has been used traditionally, and when we think you'd be wasting your money. For anything medical, talk to your doctor, especially if you're pregnant or nursing, have a diagnosed condition, or take prescription medication.

Are you a dispensary?

We’re a neighborhood wellness boutique. This website’s shopping features cover the ordinary merchandise identified in our online catalog.

For questions about the store or a specific product, call (312) 375-4448.

Do I have to buy something?

No. People stand at the jar wall for twenty minutes and leave with nothing. That's a good visit. Come in out of the cold and ask a question.

How old do I have to be, and will you check?

21 and over. We check ID for anyone who appears under 30, every single time, with no exceptions. That includes busy nights, and it includes regulars.

There's a university half a mile away. We don't bend on this one.
