Straight answers

Ask the
Alchemist.

The questions people actually ask at the counter.

I've never been in a shop like this. Where do I start?

With a feeling, not a product. Say "I want to focus," or "I want to wind down," or "no idea, show me something." We hear all three every day.

You don't need to know a brand or an ingredient name. That's our job.

Is this the real thing, or is it fake?

Everything we carry is third-party lab tested, and every batch has a certificate of analysis with a scannable code on the package.

Ask and we'll pull it up at the counter while you stand there. That isn't a favor, it's the minimum.

What is a certificate of analysis?

A report from an independent laboratory, not the brand and not us, showing what a batch actually contains and that it was screened for contaminants.

Bring us any label, from anywhere, and we'll read it with you.

Are the mushrooms psychedelic?

No. Everything on our mushroom shelf is a functional or culinary mushroom: Lion's Mane, Cordyceps, Reishi and Chaga. Same category of thing as a shiitake. Food and traditional herbal ingredients, sold as dietary supplements.

We do not sell psilocybin or anything psychedelic, and we never will. Psilocybin is a controlled substance in Illinois.

Can you tell me what to take for my condition?

No, and we'd be suspicious of any shop that said yes. We're a retail store, not a clinic, and nothing we sell is intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease.

What we can do is tell you what something is, where it came from, how it has been used traditionally, and when we think you'd be wasting your money. For anything medical, talk to your doctor, especially if you're pregnant or nursing, have a diagnosed condition, or take prescription medication.

Are you a dispensary?

No. Urban Alchemist is not a licensed cannabis dispensary and we don't sell cannabis as defined by Illinois law. We're a wellness boutique carrying functional mushrooms, nootropics, teas, botanicals, glass and snacks. We say it plainly because the difference matters.

Do I have to buy something?

No. People stand at the jar wall for twenty minutes and leave with nothing. That's a good visit. Come in out of the cold and ask a question.

How old do I have to be, and will you check?

21 and over. We check ID for anyone who appears under 30, every single time, with no exceptions. That includes busy nights, and it includes regulars.

There's a university half a mile away. We don't bend on this one.
